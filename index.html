01 / About

A business lens.
A data science toolkit.

My experience at Ford Pro Charging has shaped the questions I want to answer: Which vehicles are good candidates for electrification? What infrastructure will they need? What might it cost?

I combine experience in product and project work with Python, SQL, machine learning, and data visualization. I enjoy taking a broad business problem, working through the data, and explaining what the results mean for the next decision.

This portfolio brings together work in fleet planning, predictive analytics, natural language processing, and visual storytelling. Each project links to a summary and its supporting artifacts. Projects represent my own analysis and are not official Ford guidance.
